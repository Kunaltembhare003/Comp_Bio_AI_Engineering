Summing the per-topic hour estimates gives ≈350 h of core work + ≈75 h across the two parallel tracks ≈ 425 h total. At ~10 focused hrs/week that is 35 weeks ≈ 8 months for the core sequence — so the original month labels are kept for continuity, but treat each as a chapter you finish, not a calendar month you survive. The two parallel tracks (AI Engineering, Cloud) are drip-fed alongside the core chapters, not done up front. Units are sized to fit one week each: where a topic ran long it is split (5 / 5b, 14a / 14b / 14c), so no unit exceeds ~12 h. If you are time-boxed and targeting a specific role, the shortest defensible path is: Chapter 1 → 2 → Sequencing Data → Statistical Genetics → Translational Biomarker → Capstone, treating Genomic FMs and Drug Discovery as read-only depth you can discuss but haven't built.
